# Profile setup for distribution and conversion

Why it matters: LinkedIn's 2026 ranker uses profile fields (industry, skills, experience, geography) as inputs [P]. A profile that clearly states the author's topic helps the feed place their posts in front of the right people. It also turns post viewers into followers. Most specific tactics below are creator or vendor advice [B]. LinkedIn publishes no uplift numbers for them.

## Headline (220 characters)
**Formula:** `[Role/identity] | [What you help whom achieve] | [Proof or distinctive angle]`

Examples:
- "Founder @ Acme | Helping B2B SaaS teams cut churn | 31% churn reduction across 40 clients"
- "Staff Engineer | Writing about platform engineering and developer productivity | Ex-Stripe"

Rules:
- Use the keywords your audience would search for.
- Keep it on your 1-3 content topics.
- Test a version for about two weeks, then compare profile views.

## About section
- The first ~200 characters show before "see more", so open with who you help and how. Don't start with your life story.
- Write in the first person, not third person.
- Include proof (results, clients, numbers), what you post about, and one clear CTA (newsletter, contact, or follow).

## Featured section
- Pin 3 items or so: your best-performing post or carousel, a lead magnet or newsletter, and a proof asset (case study or talk).
- Swap items out as better posts emerge.

## Follow vs Connect
- The Creator Mode toggle was removed in 2024, and its features are now defaults for eligible profiles.
- You can make **Follow** the primary profile button in your settings (Visibility / Followers). Do this if the user posts consistently and wants audience growth.
- An inactive "creator" profile looks abandoned. Only push Follow if the user will keep posting.

## Skills and experience
- List skills that match the content topics, since skills are a ranking input.
- Experience entries should use the same vocabulary as the posts.

## Topic consistency
- Pick 1-3 pillars. Most posts, ideally 80% or more, should sit inside them. The headline, About, skills, and Featured section should all point the same way.
- Off-topic posts can still do well. But a consistent profile-topic match is the best available lever, given how the 2026 ranking system matches posts to readers.

## Company pages
- Company page posts reach about 1.6% of followers, and personal profiles get far more reach [S, van der Blom 2025].
- Strategy: founders and employees post from personal profiles, and the company page reshares and hosts evergreen content and job posts. Use the page's "notify employees" feature sparingly.
