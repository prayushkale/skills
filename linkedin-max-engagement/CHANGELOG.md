# Changelog

## 2026-11-04 — v1.1 (eval fixes)
- Banned the negation-contrast pattern in all its forms and added a final self-check that searches drafts for it.
- The hook line is now explicitly limited to 10 words, counted.
- A carousel's last slide now has exactly one request verb (no save + follow or save + question).

## 2026-11-03 — v1
- Initial skill, built from research on the official March 2026 LinkedIn feed posts (LLM retrieval, Generative Recommender, enforcement against pods, automated comments, and comment bait), the 2024 dwell-time post, and data studies from van der Blom, AuthoredUp, Socialinsider, Buffer, Metricool, ContentIn, Ordinal, LigoSocial, and SEOWorks.
- Workflow: intake → format by goal → draft → 16-point scorecard → 2-3 variants plus a post-publish plan. Includes a carousel slide-by-slide workflow and a comment workflow.
- References cover algorithm, hooks-and-writing, formats, comments-and-growth, profile, penalties, and sources, with each claim tagged by evidence tier.
- Evals in `claude plugin eval` format.
