# Pre-generation checklist

Answer every item yes or no. Fix every "no" before you spend credits.

1. A lane (A/B/C) was chosen, and the **use** is written in one sentence.
2. The hook is at most 2 short lines, with the **title in line 1**.
3. There's **one repeated chant word or syllable** where the move or the cut lands.
4. The hook is sung within the **first 15 s**, and the first section is not a verse.
5. The hook comes back as the chorus (by about 25 s in a song under 90 s).
6. The song **ends on the hook**, on the beat, with no fade or fragment, so it loops.
7. The tempo is written as a number in the lane's range (A: 102–128, B: 90–130, C: 95–125).
8. The energy is stated: steady high (A), build-to-drop (B), or build-to-line (C).
9. The tags or prompt number 6–10, not a pile of flavour words.
10. The lyrics are clean, and no real artist or voice is named.
11. The word count fits the length (about 2–2.5 sung words per second).
12. There's no bridge in a song under 90 s.
