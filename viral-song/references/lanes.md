# Lanes and the base spec

Source: the recipe population, 340 songs that went viral in 2010 or later and that a new song
could plausibly copy. All tempo, mood, energy and hook traits are a vision model's reading of
spectrograms, so treat them as soft. There's no key data. Tempo is known for only 151 of the 340.

## Base spec (all lanes)
| Ingredient | Default | Hits sharing it |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Sung vocal melody | 72% |
| Loop | The hook section loops cleanly | 71% high loopability |
| Energy | Flat-high, or build-to-drop | 45% / 30% |
| Tempo | 100–128 BPM (median 115) | known for 151 |
| First chorus | Early (median 32 s; middle half 13–52 s) | — |
| Bridge | Optional | 64% have none |
| Title | In the chorus | 64% |
| Explicit | Clean | 97% |
| Genre | Pop, or electronic dance | 42% / 25% |
| Theme | Love/desire, party, heartbreak | 29% / 11% / 10% |
| Platform | TikTok-first | 54% |

## A. Dance challenge (n = 101, the largest lane)
- **Hook:** a beat pattern (58%) plus a sung (57%) or chanted (25%) hook. 21% have the hook in the first 5 s.
- **First chorus:** median 28 s (middle half 8.5–42 s).
- **Energy:** flat-high 58%, build-to-drop 30%. No quiet passages. High loopability in 76%.
- **Mood:** euphoric 50%, happy 38%.
- **Genre:** pop, dance, hip-hop, Latin, Afrobeats-pop.
- **Tempo:** median 117.5 BPM (102–128).
- **Theme:** love/desire, party, playful nonsense. Title in the chorus for 67%.
- **Lead voice:** female and male equally.

## B. Edit sound (n = 74)
- **Use:** a transition edit (81%), a speed edit (32%).
- **Energy:** build-to-drop 35%, sawtooth 20%. Write a half-second of silence before the hit.
- **Mood:** melancholic/nostalgic 45%, dark 42%.
- **Vocal:** a male lead (49%) is common, and processed/pitched vocals appear often.
- **Visuals:** static art is enough.
- **Timing:** it is often a slow burn; the median song went viral 3 years after release.
- **Versions:** release sped-up and slowed versions as well.

## C. Lip-sync line (n = 27, the least certain)
- **Use:** one quotable line, 6–10 words, that people want to claim as their own feeling (a lip-sync quote in 63%).
- **Hook:** a vocal melody (85%) that carries the line; the energy builds into it.
- **Mood:** dark, melancholic or chill.
- **Tempo:** median 105 BPM.
- **Structure:** put the line first in the chorus, and end the song on it alone.

## Not a target
- **Streaming megahit:** 99% charted, and only 10% were TikTok-first.
- **Meme sound:** 100% rode someone else's template.

The full numbers and the 703-song analysis behind them are at https://hitlab.prayush.dev (the Recipe tab).
